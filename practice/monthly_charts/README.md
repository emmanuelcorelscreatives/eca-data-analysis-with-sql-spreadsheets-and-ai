This constructed practice fixture reproduces the exact eight rows in lesson 27. Each row is one sales line. Amounts in this exercise are explicitly denominated in USD; this defines the unit of the synthetic teaching fixture and makes no claim about an observed business currency.

Import sales_working.csv at Sales_working!A1. Build the lesson 27 pivot by reporting_month and category, summing line_sales. Independently calculated monthly values (Apparel, Home) are January (50, 50), February (40, 70), and March (25, 15). Monthly totals are 100, 110, and 40. The three observed pairs are (50, 50), (40, 70), and (25, 15). Title it Monthly revenue relationship: Apparel and Home; label the axes Apparel revenue (USD) and Home revenue (USD).

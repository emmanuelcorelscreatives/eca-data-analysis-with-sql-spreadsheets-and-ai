Worked example: Ask which product categories had the largest decrease in recorded item sales from October–December 2024 to January–March 2025. The audience is a retail merchandising manager deciding which category declines need investigation. Use order items linked to orders in those windows, placed by order date. Define recorded item sales as item quantity times recorded unit price, before returns and tax. Present one row per category with both quarter totals and the current-minus-earlier change. Require complete, nonoverlapping windows; category totals that reconcile to eligible-item totals or have explained differences; one final row per category; and workbook figures that match the checked extract. Confirm source coverage, price-field availability, and category mapping before committing to the analysis.

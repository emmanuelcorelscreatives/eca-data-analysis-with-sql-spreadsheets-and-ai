Constructed example—not retailer data. Small: 1 flagged / 1 eligible = 100%. Large: 3 flagged / 9 eligible = 33.33% rounded. Equal-group average of the unrounded rates = 66.67% rounded. Overall share of eligible orders flagged = (1 + 3) / (1 + 9) = 4 / 10 = 40%. The ratio of totals is appropriate only when the groups' numerator and denominator rules match and their counts can be added without overlap. Zero flagged / five eligible = 0%; zero eligible leaves the rate undefined.

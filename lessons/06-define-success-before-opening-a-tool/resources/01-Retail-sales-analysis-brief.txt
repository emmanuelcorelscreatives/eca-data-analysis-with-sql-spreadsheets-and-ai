RETAIL SALES ANALYSIS BRIEF — PROPOSED, NOT ANALYZED

Audience: Retail operations manager.
Decision: Decide where to investigate a possible sales decline. Do not treat an observed change as proof of its cause.

Proposed reporting window: January 1–March 31, 2025.
Population: Orders in that window by recorded order date, if the date field is suitable. If coverage is incomplete, agree a replacement window before analysis.

Questions
1. How does recorded sales value change month by month in the window?
2. Which product categories contribute the most recorded sales value in the same window?
3. How many distinct customers placed orders in each month of the window?

Acceptance criteria
- Deliver one clearly labeled answer per question, showing its period and population.
- Proposed sales value: item quantity multiplied by recorded unit price, if those fields are available. This is not approved as net revenue. Agree how returns and cancellations will be treated before drawing conclusions.
- Count distinct customers for question 3, not order rows.
- Confirm period coverage and identify incomplete months.
- Report missing order dates, customer identifiers, and product categories among relevant records.
- Reconcile monthly and category sales to the same eligible item-level total; account for missing categories. State exclusions and use the approved population and measure.

Limits and decisions to resolve
- Available date coverage has not been confirmed.
- A consistent treatment of returns and cancellations has not been established.
- Category labels or customer identifiers may be missing; report the extent rather than assuming a rate.
- Transaction records alone cannot establish why shopper behavior changed.
- If a required field or approved definition is unavailable, document the gap and agree a narrower question before presenting a result.

Review decision: Approve or revise the window, population, proposed measure, and unresolved treatments before analysis.

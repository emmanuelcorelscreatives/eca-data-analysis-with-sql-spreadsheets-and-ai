ILLUSTRATIVE RETAIL EXAMPLE — NOT ANALYSIS RESULTS
Reader: Retail sales manager
Decision: Which product category should we investigate first for a revenue decline?
When: Monthly review after the latest complete month
Action if unclear: Defer ranking and ask for data coverage or definition review
1 | Are periods and coverage comparable? | Establish whether a fair comparison is possible
2 | Did defined revenue fall, and by how much? | Test the premise and establish the size of the change
3 | Which categories contributed most to the change? | Identify candidates for investigation
Outside scope: Why revenue changed; which campaign to launch
Success check: Reader can name a category to investigate, or state why selection must wait

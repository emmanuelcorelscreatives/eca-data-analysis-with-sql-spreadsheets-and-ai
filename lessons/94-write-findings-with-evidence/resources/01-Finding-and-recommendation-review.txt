For each finding:

[ ] State the direction and size of the result; identify the base of any percentage.
[ ] Cite the exact location and version of the previously validated result.
[ ] Explain the decision-relevant implication without claiming an untested cause.
[ ] Keep material coverage and measure limitations beside the claim.

[ ] Remove or qualify words such as “because,” “always,” and “proves” unless the evidence supports them.

Draft pattern:
Finding: In [defined population], [measure] was [value] in [period] versus [value] in [comparison period], a [difference] ([percentage with stated base]).
Evidence: [Exact validated result location and relevant breakdown].
Implication: [What this checked comparison helps the reader prioritize].
Limit: [Material boundary on this interpretation].
Recommendation: [Proposed next step, explicitly not an established fact].

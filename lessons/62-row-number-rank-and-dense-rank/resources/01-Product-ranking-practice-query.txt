PostgreSQL reference: https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/functions-window.html

Run in pgAdmin 4 Query Tool:

WITH product_totals (product_id, product_name, revenue) AS (
  VALUES
    (101, 'Kettle', 240.00),
    (102, 'Lamp', 240.00),
    (103, 'Mug', 180.00),
    (104, 'Tray', 120.00),
    (105, 'Vase', 120.00)
)
SELECT
  product_id,
  product_name,
  revenue,
  ROW_NUMBER() OVER (ORDER BY revenue DESC, product_id ASC) AS display_position,
  RANK() OVER (ORDER BY revenue DESC) AS rank_position,
  DENSE_RANK() OVER (ORDER BY revenue DESC) AS dense_position
FROM product_totals
ORDER BY revenue DESC, product_id ASC;

Expected rows (product_id | product_name | revenue | display_position | rank_position | dense_position):
101 | Kettle | 240.00 | 1 | 1 | 1
102 | Lamp | 240.00 | 2 | 1 | 1
103 | Mug | 180.00 | 3 | 3 | 2
104 | Tray | 120.00 | 4 | 4 | 3
105 | Vase | 120.00 | 5 | 4 | 3

Check: tied revenues share RANK and DENSE_RANK values. RANK skips positions after ties; DENSE_RANK does not. The distinct product_id breaks ties only for ROW_NUMBER. When adapting the query, use a stable, unique product identifier for individual ordering.
